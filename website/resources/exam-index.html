Scope and verification notes

  • Question references were taken from the five uploaded examination papers. On the scanned 2022 and 2023 papers, some questions span pages or contain options/figures as images; the page ranges point to the question and its immediate continuation.
  • This is an index, so descriptions are deliberately shorter than the original questions and do not supply solutions.
  • Priorities reflect your stated focus (Semester Test 2, Lectures 11–18) and the patterns in these papers, rather than official test-topic guarantees.
